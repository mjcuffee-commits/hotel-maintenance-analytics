# January 2027 Operations BI Roadmap

## Mission

Become interview-ready for an **Operations Business Intelligence Analyst** position by January 31, 2027, and compete for internships and early-career analyst roles in aviation, technical operations, hospitality, logistics, and facilities.

This roadmap treats the hotel maintenance project as the main proof of technical ability and operations knowledge.

## Target Role

Primary title:

- Operations Business Intelligence Analyst

Closely related search titles:

- Operations Analytics Analyst
- Technical Operations Analyst
- Maintenance Analytics Analyst
- Reliability Data Analyst
- Business Intelligence Analyst

The goal is one career lane with several employer-specific titles—not several unrelated career paths.

## Professional Positioning

> I am a U.S. Army veteran and former hotel engineer completing Georgia State University's M.S. in Analytics. I use SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze maintenance and operational performance, with a focus on downtime, cost, service levels, repeat failures, and business decisions.

## Non-Negotiable Weekly Commitments

Every week:

- Complete three focused SQL practice sessions.
- Advance the Power BI dashboard by at least one measurable step.
- Complete two Python/pandas business-analysis sessions.
- Independently explain or rebuild one part of this project.
- Contact five Georgia State alumni, employer representatives, or veterans.
- Submit five to ten carefully selected applications.
- Follow up with every meaningful event connection within 24 hours.
- Protect class attendance, assignments, exam preparation, and graduate GPA.

## Definition of Interview-Ready

The January goal is complete when all of the following are true:

- [ ] I can write joins, aggregations, CTEs, `CASE` expressions, and window functions without copying an answer.
- [ ] I can build and explain a professional Power BI dashboard from raw data.
- [ ] I can clean, merge, group, and visualize data using pandas.
- [ ] I can run this project from beginning to end and explain what every major step does.
- [ ] I can distinguish an observation from a causal conclusion.
- [ ] I can present the problem, analysis, findings, recommendations, and limitations in five minutes.
- [ ] I have a consistent record of networking, applications, and timely follow-up.
- [ ] My academic responsibilities remain on schedule.

## Honesty Standard

Using documentation, instructors, classmates, and AI for learning is acceptable. Presenting work that cannot be independently explained, changed, or reproduced is not. Every portfolio claim must be supported by personal understanding.
