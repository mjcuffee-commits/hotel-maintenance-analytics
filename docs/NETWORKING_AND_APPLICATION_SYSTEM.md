# Networking and Application System

## Weekly Targets

- Five quality professional contacts
- Five to ten carefully selected applications
- Follow-up within 24 hours after every meaningful event conversation
- One informational conversation whenever possible
- One weekly review of responses, interviews, and next actions

Quality matters more than sending the largest number of messages or applications.

## Before a Georgia State or Veteran Event

1. Identify three priority employers.
2. Find one relevant operations, analytics, aviation, logistics, facilities, or hospitality role at each employer.
3. Prepare one informed question for each organization.
4. Practice the professional introduction twice.
5. Make the next requested action simple: a LinkedIn connection, introduction, or short informational conversation.

## Professional Introduction

> I am a U.S. Army veteran and former hotel engineer completing Georgia State University's M.S. in Analytics. I use SQL, Python, and Power BI to analyze maintenance and operational performance. I am targeting operations business intelligence roles where I can help reduce downtime, control costs, and improve service performance.

## Useful Event Questions

- Which team handles operations reporting or business intelligence at your organization?
- What would make a graduate candidate stand out for that team?
- Which technical skill does your team use most frequently?
- Would you recommend anyone in operations analytics or technical operations for me to learn from?

## Follow-Up Within 24 Hours

> Hello [Name], it was great meeting you at [event]. I appreciated your advice about [specific topic]. My Army HVAC and hotel-engineering background is what led me toward operations analytics, and I am currently strengthening my SQL, Python, and Power BI skills at Georgia State. I would be glad to stay connected.

## Informational Conversation Request

> Thank you for connecting with me. Your work in [team or field] closely relates to the operations BI path I am pursuing. If you have 15 minutes in the next two weeks, I would appreciate hearing how your team uses data and what you would recommend I demonstrate as a graduate candidate.

## Referral Standard

Ask for a referral only after the person understands the target role and has had an opportunity to evaluate the background or project.

> Based on what we discussed, do you think my background aligns with this position? If so, would you feel comfortable referring me or directing me to the appropriate recruiter?

## Application Quality Check

Before submitting:

- [ ] The role belongs to the Operations BI career lane.
- [ ] The resume uses the employer's truthful language for relevant skills.
- [ ] The strongest operations and analytics evidence appears near the top.
- [ ] The project link works.
- [ ] The application contains no unfinished placeholders.
- [ ] Employment dates and claims are accurate.
- [ ] A follow-up date is recorded.

## Weekly Review

At the end of each week, record:

- Applications submitted
- Warm connections created
- Follow-ups completed on time
- Recruiter responses
- Informational conversations
- Interviews scheduled
- Technical weaknesses discovered
- One improvement for the following week

## Privacy

This repository is public. Do not commit private email addresses, phone numbers, disability documents, application credentials, or personal notes about professional contacts. Keep completed trackers in a private location; the repository contains blank templates only.
